About Me

Science, Code &
Curiosity.

The intersection of biology and technology is where I thrive.

I'm Dipesh Kharel, a student at Cosmic International Academy pursuing Science (Biology), while simultaneously building web experiences that push boundaries. Currently in 2026, I'm sharpening both my scientific thinking and my engineering intuition.

My work ranges from AI-powered web tools to philosophical digital experiences — projects that reflect both analytical rigor and creative ambition. I'm particularly drawn to the potential of artificial intelligence as a medium for human connection.

When I'm not dissecting code or organisms, you'll find me exploring ideas at the edge of what software can do — building tools I'd actually want to use.
